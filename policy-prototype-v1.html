Leave of absence policy

This policy sets out how students on taught programmes can take a break from their studies and how this is managed by the University.

1. Purpose

Students may sometimes need to take a break from their studies because of health, personal or other circumstances. This section explains the purpose of the policy and the framework used to manage leave of absence.

2. Definition

2.1 A leave of absence is an approved break from a taught programme for a specified period.

2.2 During the approved period, normal academic requirements are paused and the student temporarily steps away from their programme.

3. Scope

3.1 The policy applies to undergraduate and taught postgraduate students, subject to the exceptions and specific requirements set out below.

  • Short-term absence may be covered by separate attendance guidance.
  • Postgraduate research students use separate leave-of-absence guidance.
  • Additional requirements can apply to sponsored, apprenticeship or professionally regulated programmes.

4. Principles

The University aims to support requests where possible while considering the student's circumstances, academic progress and any practical implications of taking a break.

5. Duration of leave of absence

A request would normally cover no more than one academic year. Further or exceptional periods may require additional approval.

6. Key implications for students

Before applying, students should consider how a break may affect areas such as:

  • tuition fees and student finance
  • immigration and visa status
  • student accommodation
  • programme structure and assessment

Read the related student guidance for practical advice and sources of support.

7. Procedure for requesting and approval

Students should first discuss their circumstances with their school, review the implications, and then follow the University's application process.

8. Status and access to University services

Students on leave are not normally expected to study or undertake assessment. Access to some University services may continue while other services are restricted.

9. Returning from a leave of absence

Ahead of the planned return date, students receive guidance about re-registration and any requirements they need to meet before resuming study.
