Not sure where to start?

Applying for a masters is different to applying for an undergraduate course.

Everything you need to know is on our applying essentials web page.

Our application process:

  1. Apply directly to us via the online application form on our website.
  2. Put your application in by Friday 3 September 2027 (for most courses starting September 2027).
  3. Include proof of your previous degree (or your latest transcript if you haven’t graduated yet).
  4. Expect a decision up to four weeks after you’ve applied. Once we’ve made a decision, it’ll be visible in your online application and you’ll receive an email confirmation.
