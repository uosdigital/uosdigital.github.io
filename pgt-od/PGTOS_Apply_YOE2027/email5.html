5 reasons to study a masters at Sheffield

1. Word-class reputation - We are a member of the UK’s prestigious Russell Group and ranked 82nd in the world by the QS World University Rankings 2027.

2. The UK’s greenest city - And the 2nd greenest in Europe after Oslo. 61% of Sheffield is green space and the Peak District National Park is just a short train or bus ride away.

3. Cost of living - Sheffield is one of the UK’s most affordable major cities to live in, and significantly cheaper than London.

4. Guaranteed accommodation - With all bills and 24/7 security included in the price. You just need to apply before our guarantee deadline.

5. Welcoming and diverse - Sheffield was the UK’s first City of Sanctuary, providing a safe space to asylum seekers and refugees. Our University welcomes students from over 150 countries.

How to apply

You can apply via the online application form on our website. As part of this process, you will be asked to provide:

  • Your most recent transcript (if currently studying)
  • Evidence of your qualifications (for completed degrees)
  • English language qualifications (if required)
  • Official translations of any documents not in English

Read our applying essentials

It typically takes our academic selectors up to four weeks to make a decision once you have applied.
