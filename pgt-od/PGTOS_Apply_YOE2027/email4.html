How do I apply?

Apply directly to us via the online application form on our website.

There is no fee to apply and you can submit applications for up to three courses at once.

Apply now

When can I apply?

You can apply for courses starting in September 2027 now. If you’re still studying, you can apply before you finish your current course.

You can find our key dates and deadlines online.

What do I need to apply? 

As part of your application, you will be asked to provide:

  • Recent transcripts (if you are currently studying)
  • Evidence of your qualifications (if you have completed your degree)
  • English language qualifications (if required)
  • Official translations of any documents not in English

Visit our website to find out more about our English language requirements and English Language Teaching Centre.

When will I hear back?

It typically takes our academic selectors up to four weeks to make a decision once you have applied.

Once we have made a decision, it will be visible in your online application and you’ll receive an email confirmation. 

That’s it! We look forward to receiving your application.
