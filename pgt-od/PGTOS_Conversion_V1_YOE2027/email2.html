3 ways we support your future success

1. Gain key skills for employers

We work closely with industry leaders - in the UK and internationally - to design our programmes. This ensures you graduate with the skills most prized by employers. Gain experience working in the UK through exclusive internships, placements and work opportunities.

2) International Career Fast Track

This is an exclusive one-year scheme designed by career experts for international students like you. It includes: exclusive employer events; one-to-one career advice appointments; virtual work experience programmes from global employers; and the opportunity to qualify for paid internships with Sheffield-based organisations.

3) Apply for a Graduate visa

The UK’s graduate visa allows you to stay and work - or look for work - after completing your course. If you decide to apply, we can provide support with the application process. You are also able to work part-time on your student visa.
