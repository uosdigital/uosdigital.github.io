Postgraduate life is very different from undergraduate study.

The academic level and expectations are higher. Many students compare it to having a full-time job.

But don’t worry - you’ll find your new routine in no time!

Some of our current postgrads share what life is like for them:

Instagram: Postgraduate life

A day on campus

Campus study spaces - There are over 4,000 dedicated study spaces on campus - from individual booths to group work spaces. Some spaces can be pre-booked, and areas are divided into general, quiet or silent study.

Goodwin Sports Centre - Our recently refurbished 180-piece gym is open 6am to 10pm on weekdays. You can also book fitness classes, courts and pitches. Memberships start at £19.99 for the first three months, and £26.99 thereafter.

Cycle commuting - The Hicks Cycle Hub provides secure card-access bike storage with lockers and showers. The Cycle Hut near the Engineering Heartspace offers drop-in bike maintenance on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Eating and drinking - There are over 20 different cafes and eating places on campus. You can earn 5p for every pound you spend in University venues with the GeniUS scheme. All Students’ Union (SU) venues are run by the SU.

What support can I access?

  • University Health Service - An on-campus NHS GP service just for University of Sheffield students
  • Students’ Union nursery - Early years on-campus provision for children aged 6 months to 5 years old
  • Mental health - Our own student mental health, counselling, therapies & wellbeing service
  • Disability and dyslexia - Confidential advice, support and personal learning support plans
  • Academic skills - Workshops in academic writing and study skills, plus one-to-one appointments
  • Student Advice Service - An independent and confidential SU service, including academic issues

Finding your community

With smaller year groups and more intense study, you’ll get to know everyone on your programme pretty quickly. But there are lots of ways to meet people outside of that.

A dedicated Postgraduate Welcome runs social meet-ups in the first two weeks of October.

The Postgraduate Society offers regular socials and events. And you can also join one of over 400 student societies and sports clubs.
