5 ways we set you up for success

1. Essential graduate skills
We work closely with industry leaders and public sector employers to design every one of our programmes. You’ll graduate with the key skills you’ll need in your future career.

2. Personalised careers advice
Book one-to-one appointments with careers advisors, and take advantage of specialist knowledge through your faculty’s own employability team.

3. Comprehensive application support
Polish your CV in guided workshops and prepare for interviews with personal coaching. Mock assessment centres simulate common graduate recruitment methods.

4. Exclusive work opportunities
Access exclusive jobs, placements and internships through our own careers portal. Or sign up for virtual work experience provided by our global partners.

5. Lifelong support
There’s no time limit on our careers and employability support. You can use our services for as long as you need to after you graduate.
