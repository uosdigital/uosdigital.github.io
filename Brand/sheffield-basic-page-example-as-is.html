City of Sheffield

Explore a city of independent neighbourhoods, culture and green spaces, with countryside close to campus.

Where’s Sheffield?

Sheffield sits between northern England and the Midlands, combining city life with easy access to the Peak District.

What’s Sheffield like?

  • Independent neighbourhoods
  • A welcoming student community
  • Parks and outdoor adventures

Things to do in Sheffield

Arts and culture

Discover theatres, music venues, festivals and independent cinemas across the city.

Eating and drinking

Explore cafés, food halls and restaurants in the city centre and surrounding neighbourhoods.

Getting outdoors

Make time for the city’s parks or plan a day in the Peak District.

Getting around

Walk, cycle or use the bus and tram to explore Sheffield. Rail connections make it easy to travel further afield.
